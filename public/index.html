Open MortalArcade

The real 2004 OpenMortal, running in your browser. Put yourself in the game, then settle it on the big screen.

Start the big screenRuns the game on this computer. Players join with their phones. Join a fightUse your phone, laptop or gamepad as a controller. Make a fighterFilm your moves, cut out the background, paint your special. Build a stagePaint or photograph a place to fight. Why this game mattersMortal Kombat, the ESRB, esports, and the joy of open source.

OpenMortal 0.7 by UPi and the OpenMortal team (GPL), running unmodified inside Boxedwine, the open-source Windows layer compiled to WebAssembly. Made for the uOttawa Game Development Club.
